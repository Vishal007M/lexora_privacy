Introduction

Welcome to Lexora. We built this app with one goal: to deliver a clean, powerful, and private learning experience — without selling your data, tracking your personal habits, or storing your personal communications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the controls you have over your data. By using Lexora, you agree to this policy.

🛡️
Data We Do NOT Collect

Lexora does not collect, transmit, or share the following:

  • ✕ Your personal identity, names, or contact lists
  • ✕ Your private chat messages or external app contents
  • ✕ Passwords, keystrokes, or login credentials
  • ✕ Location data, GPS, camera, or microphone access
  • ✕ Device identifiers for tracking across apps
  • ✕ Any personal user data that leaves your device
💾
Data Stored Locally On Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device inside a secure local Room SQLite database and DataStore, and is never uploaded to our servers or third parties:

  • ✓ Define with Lexora History: When you use the instant definition feature, your recent looked-up words are kept in a local history list for your convenience so you can revisit them later. You can clear this history anytime.
  • ✓ Language Preferences: Your selected learning language and translation/primary mother tongue.
  • ✓ Custom Word Collections & Favorites: Word collections, custom folders, and bookmarked terms created by you.
  • ✓ Learning Stats & Streaks: Local quiz progress, daily streak counts, and flashcard mastery.

💡 You have complete control: You can clear history or reset app data at any time from within the app settings.

🌐
Third-Party Services

To provide language translations and optional app monetization, Lexora interacts with trusted standard service providers:

  • ✓ Language Translation Services: Word definitions and translations are fetched via secure APIs strictly for language learning. No personal identifiers or user tracking data are attached to translation queries.
  • ✓ Google AdMob: Standard non-intrusive mobile ads powered by the official Google Mobile Ads SDK, strictly following Google Play Developer Policies and Families requirements.

5.1. AccessibilityService API Policy Disclosure

Lexora utilizes the Android AccessibilityService API to provide its optional, user-initiated "Define with Lexora" floating lookup functionality.

  • ✓ How It Is Used: When enabled by the user, the Accessibility Service observes text selection events across other apps (e.g. messaging, reading, browsing) to automatically trigger an instant floating definition bubble without requiring the user to switch apps.
  • ✓ Data Handled: Only the specifically selected or highlighted word is read into temporary memory to fetch definitions from dictionary services.
  • ✓ Zero Personal Data Stored or Shared: Lexora does NOT collect, store, log, or transmit any user keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or sensitive information.
  • ✓ User Control: This permission is completely optional, requires explicit user consent, and can be disabled at any time from within Lexora Settings or Android Accessibility Settings.
🕒
5.2. Define with Lexora History Privacy

When words are looked up via the "Define with Lexora" floating bubble or system text action, they are stored locally on your device for your reference:

  • ✓ Stored Exclusively On-Device: Selected words, part of speech, definition snippets, and translations are kept in a local encrypted SQLite database on your device.
  • ✓ Zero Tracking or External Transmission: History entries are never uploaded to any cloud server, third party, or analytics engine.
  • ✓ User Deletion: You can delete any individual history item or clear the entire history log at any time directly from the History screen or from Settings.
🌐
5.3. Multi-Language Translation Data Privacy

Lexora allows users to learn vocabulary and view translations in their mother tongue / primary language:

  • ✓ No User Profiling: Translation queries consist strictly of the vocabulary word or educational phrase. Queries are never linked to your identity, IP profile, or device ID.
  • ✓ Local Caching: Frequently translated words are cached locally in your device's SQLite database to minimize network usage and provide instant access.
🔊
5.4. Text-to-Speech & Audio Pronunciations

Audio pronunciations in Lexora are powered by the on-device Android TextToSpeech engine. Lexora does not access the microphone, record user voice input, or send audio data to any external server.
